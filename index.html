🎒 The Catholic Disney Park Survival Guide

🍽️ Saying Grace in Disney Dining

Don't be shy to make the Sign of the Cross and say the traditional Catholic blessing before meals at table service or quick-service spots. It is a peaceful witness of faith.

🕊️ Fasting on Ash Wednesday & Lent

Visiting during Lent? Columbia Harbour House (Magic Kingdom) and Epcot's fish options make Friday abstinence simple. Plan simple meals on fasting days.

🛡️ The Catholic Parent Discernment Framework

Rather than viewing media through pure cynicism or uncritical acceptance, the Catholic tradition calls us to 'test everything; hold fast what is good' (1 Thess 5:21). We evaluate storytelling through four classical Catholic lenses:

1. Truth (Veritas)

Does the story reflect the true nature of the human person, objective moral consequences, and the reality of good and evil?

2. Goodness (Bonum)

Are virtues (courage, sacrifice, purity, forgiveness) praised, while vices (greed, pride, deceit) are recognized as destructive?

3. Beauty (Pulchrum)

Does the artistry, musical composition, and visual imagination evoke wonder that points the heart upward toward the Divine Artist?

4. Family Unity (Communio)

Does the story honor the dignity of marriage, fatherhood, motherhood, and reconciliation among siblings?
