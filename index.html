🎒 Catholic Family Park Tips & Blessings

📿 Sanctify Long Waits: Pray a 1-Decade Queue Rosary

A 30-to-60 minute ride line is the ideal opportunity to pray one decade with your family. It transforms restlessness into peace, offers up the Florida heat, and teaches kids to sanctify ordinary waiting time.

🍽️ Saying Grace at Disney Restaurants

Making the Sign of the Cross and saying the family blessing before meals is a joyful witness of faith right in the middle of the theme parks!

🕊️ Fasting on Ash Wednesday & Lent

Visiting during Lent? Columbia Harbour House (Magic Kingdom) and Epcot's fish dishes make Friday abstinence easy and delicious.

🚗 Patron Saint Invocations

Invoke St. Christopher for safe highway/plane travel, St. Raphael for joyful journeys, and St. Anthony for misplaced Disney pins!

🛡️ The Catholic Parent Discernment Framework

Rather than viewing media through fear or cynicism, Catholic families are called to 'test everything; hold fast what is good' (1 Thess 5:21). We evaluate stories through four classical transcendentals:

1. Truth (Veritas)

Does the story reflect true human nature, real moral consequences, and the reality of good and evil?

2. Goodness (Bonum)

Are virtues (courage, sacrifice, purity, forgiveness) celebrated, while vices (greed, pride) are shown as destructive?

3. Beauty (Pulchrum)

Does the artwork, music, and storytelling evoke wonder that lifts the heart upward toward the Divine Artist?

4. Family Unity (Communio)

Does the story honor the dignity of marriage, fatherhood, motherhood, and forgiveness among siblings?
