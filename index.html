🛡️ The Catholic Parent Discernment Framework

Rather than viewing media through fear or cynicism, Catholic families are called to 'test everything; hold fast what is good' (1 Thess 5:21). We evaluate stories through four classical transcendentals:

1. Truth (Veritas)

Does the story reflect true human nature, real moral consequences, and the reality of good and evil?

2. Goodness (Bonum)

Are virtues (courage, sacrifice, purity, forgiveness) celebrated, while vices (greed, pride) are shown as destructive?

3. Beauty (Pulchrum)

Does the artwork, music, and storytelling evoke wonder that lifts the heart upward toward the Divine Artist?

4. Family Unity (Communio)

Does the story honor the dignity of marriage, fatherhood, motherhood, and forgiveness among siblings?
